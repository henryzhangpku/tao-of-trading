# Notes and questions

## Direction (20 November 2026)

The book is about the trader's mindset. Each chapter takes something traders already "know" and turns it over through Laozi. Sections: The Verse / What You Know / The Other Way / The Story / Try This (zh: 经文 / 你以为 / 另一条路 / 故事 / 试一试). Stories are universal trading-day scenes in the second person unless Henry gives a true incident of his own. Quant and AI-desk material appears at most as a passing example. See NOTES-henry.md.

## Questions for Henry

### Prologue
- Do you want one true line about when you first read the Dao De Jing as a child (who gave it to you, school or home)? The prologue now says only that you read it "as a child in China, in the original".

### Chapter 1 (Why It Moved)
- The story is a generic scene: a stock down 4% at the open, the trader hunting for a headline, buying on it, and the price falling on. Do you have a real morning like this of your own? If so, give the rough shape and I will rewrite the story around it.

### Chapter 2 (Long and Short Make Each Other)
- The story is the generic "best day, then oversize the next morning" scene. Do you have your own version of it, or of a clean, planned loss you are proud of?

### Chapter 50 (Coming Out to Live, Going In to Die)
- Have you ever had an overnight gap go straight through a stop? A true account would beat the universal scene.
