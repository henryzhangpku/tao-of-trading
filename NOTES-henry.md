- 2026-11-16: Henry is "very influenced by" Mark Spitznagel, *The Dao of Capital: Austrian Investing in a Distorted World* (Wiley, 2013). Spitznagel isn't Chinese, but he builds the book on ancient Chinese thought: Laozi's Dao, *shi* 势 (strategic positioning), the roundabout path 迂 (*Umweg*, giving up a little now to gain more later), and the patient, indirect route over the direct grab. This sits close to "slow is fast". Use it as a touchstone for tone and structure, with the trading lens added. Cite and credit it where ideas overlap, and never copy its text.
