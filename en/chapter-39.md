# 39. Attaining the One

*Part II: The Virtue · 《昔之得一者》*

## The Verse

昔之得一者：天得一以清，地得一以宁，神得一以灵，谷得一以盈，万物得一以生，侯王得一以为天下贞。

其致之，天无以清将恐裂，地无以宁将恐发，神无以灵将恐歇，谷无以盈将恐竭，万物无以生将恐灭，侯王无以贵高将恐蹶。

故贵以贱为本，高以下为基。是以侯王自谓孤、寡、不谷。此非以贱为本邪？非乎？故致数舆无舆。不欲琭琭如玉，珞珞如石。

Of old, these attained the one. Heaven attained the one and was clear. Earth attained the one and was at rest. The spirits attained the one and were potent. The valley attained the one and was full. The ten thousand things attained the one and lived. Lords and kings attained the one and set the world right.

Pressed to the end: heaven without its clearness would split, earth without its rest would quake, the spirits without their potency would fade, the valley without its fullness would run dry, the ten thousand things without life would perish, and lords and kings without their high place would fall.

So the noble has the humble for its root, and the high has the low for its foundation. That is why lords and kings call themselves the orphan, the lonely, the unworthy. Is this not taking the humble as the root? Is it not? Count the parts of a carriage and you have no carriage. Do not wish to glitter like jade. Be plain, like stone.

*The Chinese is the received Wang Bi text. The translation is the author's own.*

## The Trader

1. Heaven attains the one and is clear. One edge, understood, is clearer than five half-understood.

2. Five strategies are not five sources of return. Often they are one exposure and four distractions.

3. Every rule you run needs its own count, its own gate, its own forward test and its own share of your attention. Attention runs out first.

4. Without its one, heaven splits. The trader with many half-rules splits too, into one rule for each mood.

5. The high has the low for its foundation. An edge rests on the dull work under it: data cleaned, costs measured, exits honoured.

6. Count the parts of a carriage and you have no carriage. Count every strategy as its own engine and you no longer know what the account is.

7. Close the side strategies. Keep the one you can explain, test, and leave alone.

8. Closing a rule is not a loss. It returns its share of attention to the rule that is left.

9. Do not glitter like jade. A short list of plain rules looks poor on a page. Be plain, like stone.
