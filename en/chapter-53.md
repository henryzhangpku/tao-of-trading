# 53. The Great Way Is Smooth

*Part II: The Virtue · 《使我介然有知》*

## The Verse

使我介然有知，行于大道，唯施是畏。大道甚夷，而民好径。

朝甚除，田甚芜，仓甚虚；服文彩，带利剑，厌饮食，财货有余；是谓盗夸。非道也哉！

If I had even a little knowledge, walking on the great way, the only thing I would fear is straying from it. The great way is very level, but people love side paths.

The court is swept very clean, the fields are choked with weeds, the granaries are empty. They wear embroidered silk, carry sharp swords, are glutted with food and drink, and have wealth to spare. This is called the boasting of robbers. It is not the way!

*The Chinese is the received Wang Bi text. The translation is the author's own.*

## The Trader

1. The great way is level: small size, a written stop, a tested rule, a long count. It is flat and dull, and it goes somewhere.

2. People love side paths: the tip, the sure thing, the position copied from someone louder.

3. A side path looks shorter because you cannot see where it ends.

4. The only thing to fear is straying. The danger is not the hard stretch of the road. It is the turn-off.

5. Copying a famous trader copies the entry and nothing else. Not the size, not the stop, not the reason, not the exit. You get the position without the plan.

6. If a public voice is worth following, it is worth logging first. Every call written down on the day it is made, misses beside hits, before a single trade follows it. Most voices do not survive their own count.

7. The court swept clean, the fields full of weeds: the handsome screen, the expensive data, the confident videos, and a ledger nobody has opened.

8. Embroidered silk and sharp swords: the account that looks rich because it is borrowed.

9. The boasting of robbers is a claimed gain with no record behind it. Distrust any boast you cannot audit, including your own.

10. It is not the way. It is only a faster road back to the start.
