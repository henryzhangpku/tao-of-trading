# 43. The Softest Thing

*Part II: The Virtue · 《天下之至柔》*

## The Verse

天下之至柔，驰骋天下之至坚。无有入无间，吾是以知无为之有益。

不言之教，无为之益，天下希及之。

The softest thing in the world gallops over the hardest. What has no substance enters where there is no gap. From this I know the benefit of not acting.

The teaching without words, the benefit of not acting: few in the world attain them.

*The Chinese is the received Wang Bi text. The translation is the author's own.*

## The Trader

1. The softest rides over the hardest. Small, repeated, defined risk outlasts any large conviction.

2. Conviction is hard. It does not bend, so when it meets something harder, it breaks, and takes the account with it.

3. A small position bends. It can be wrong many times in a row and still be there on the morning it is right.

4. Cap the size in code, not in confidence. Confidence is highest exactly when the cap is most needed.

5. The cap on the trade card is not a judgement of the idea. It is the same for the idea you love and the idea you barely believe.

6. What has no substance enters where there is no gap. A stop has no opinion. It gets out where an opinion would have stayed.

7. Water does not argue with the stone. It comes back every day, and it is the stone that changes.

8. The benefit of not acting: the position you did not double, the stop you did not move, the cap you did not lift.

9. The teaching without words: a rule that works without needing to be explained to you again each morning.

10. Few attain it, because it is boring. Boredom is the whole price, and it is cheap.
