# 16. Return to the Root

*Part I: The Way · 《致虚极，守静笃》*

## The Verse

致虚极，守静笃。万物并作，吾以观复。

夫物芸芸，各复归其根。归根曰静，是谓复命。复命曰常，知常曰明。不知常，妄作，凶。

知常容，容乃公，公乃王，王乃天，天乃道，道乃久，没身不殆。

Reach the utmost emptiness. Hold stillness firmly. The ten thousand things rise together, and I watch them return.

Things flourish in their multitudes, and each goes back to its root. Going back to the root is called stillness. Stillness is called returning to one's nature. Returning to one's nature is called the constant. Knowing the constant is called clarity. Not knowing the constant, people act wildly, and come to harm.

Knowing the constant, you can hold everything. Holding everything, you are fair. Being fair, you are kingly. Being kingly, you are like heaven. Being like heaven, you are the way. Being the way, you last. To the end of your days you are not in danger.

*The Chinese is the received Wang Bi text. The translation is the author's own.*

## The Trader

1. Every day starts flat in the mind, whatever the account says. Yesterday's trade is closed. So is yesterday's mood.

2. Reach the utmost emptiness before the open, not after. The first hour before the screen is for emptying, not for filling.

3. The ten thousand things rise together: every name, every headline, every chart moving at once. I watch them return. Most of what rises at the open is back where it started by the close.

4. Each goes back to its root. Prices return, moods return, the urge to trade returns every morning. Knowing that it returns is what lets you not obey it.

5. The root of a trading day is the routine: the same checks, in the same order, every day, before anything is decided. The routine is the stillness. It does not care what happened yesterday.

6. Knowing the constant is clarity. The constant is not the market. It is your size, your stop, your loss limit. They are the same on a good morning and a bad one.

7. Not knowing the constant, people act wildly. Wild action is not dramatic. It is a size changed because of a feeling, a stop moved because of a hope.

8. To the end of your days you are not in danger. That is the whole aim. Not the best year: the account that is still open in the last one.
