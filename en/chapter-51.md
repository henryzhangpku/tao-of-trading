# 51. The Tao Gives Birth

*Part II: The Virtue · 《道生之，德畜之》*

## The Verse

道生之，德畜之，物形之，势成之。是以万物莫不尊道而贵德。

道之尊，德之贵，夫莫之命而常自然。

故道生之，德畜之；长之育之；亭之毒之；养之覆之。生而不有，为而不恃，长而不宰，是谓玄德。

The way gives them birth. Virtue rears them. Things give them shape. Circumstance completes them. So all the ten thousand things revere the way and honour virtue.

The way is revered and virtue honoured not because anyone commands it, but always of themselves.

So the way gives birth and virtue rears; it grows them and raises them, settles them and ripens them, feeds them and shelters them. It gives life and does not possess. It acts and does not lean on what it does. It leads and does not rule. This is called the dark virtue.

*The Chinese is the received Wang Bi text. The translation is the author's own.*

## The Trader

1. You give the rule birth. Then it has to grow by itself: through the data, through the test, through the forward run.

2. Circumstance completes it. The market decides in which seasons a rule is paid. You do not.

3. Gives life and does not possess. A rule you love is a rule you will protect from the evidence.

4. Let a new rule live in the shadow first, running on paper beside the real ones, where being wrong costs nothing but pride.

5. Acts and does not lean on it. A rule's good month is not to your credit. Its bad month is not your disgrace.

6. Leads and does not rule. Set the walls before it starts: the size, the stop, the length of the test. Inside the walls, do not steer.

7. Settles them and ripens them. A rule pulled out of the shadow early is fruit picked green. It looks the same and tastes of nothing.

8. Feeds them and shelters them. Keep its data clean, keep its costs honest, and keep everyone's hands off it, your own first.

9. No one commands it. A rule that only works while you watch it is not a rule yet.

10. The dark virtue: the best systems look, from outside, as if nobody built them.
