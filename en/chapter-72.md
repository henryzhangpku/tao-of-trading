# 72. When People Do Not Fear

*Part II: The Virtue · 《民不畏威》*

## The Verse

民不畏威，则大威至。

无狎其所居，无厌其所生。夫唯不厌，是以不厌。

是以圣人自知不自见，自爱不自贵。故去彼取此。

When people no longer fear what is dreadful, the great dread arrives.

Do not crowd them in their dwellings. Do not press on their lives. Only when you do not press on them will they not turn against you.

So the sage knows himself and does not display himself. He cares for himself and does not exalt himself. So he leaves that and takes this.

*The Chinese is the received Wang Bi text. The translation is the author's own.*

## The Trader

1. When the trader stops fearing the tail, the tail arrives. Not because the market is watching, but because a trader without fear has already sized for a world without tails.

2. Calm months are when risk grows. The quiet does not reduce the danger. It reduces your memory of it, and size creeps up to fill the space.

3. So keep the protection on in the calm month, when it feels most unnecessary. The stop stays in. The size stays small. The cash stays uncommitted. Protection bought in a storm is bought at the storm's price.

4. Do not crowd the account in its dwelling. Leave room in it: no margin used to the last inch, no position so large that one gap would move into the house.

5. Do not press on its life. An account pushed to work every day, in every market, at full size, will one day push back.

6. Only when you do not press will it not turn on you. The account that is asked for little is the one still standing after the bad week.

7. The sage knows himself and does not display himself. Know your risk in detail. Do not show your returns to anyone, including yourself every hour.

8. He cares for himself and does not exalt himself. Protect yourself as if you were ordinary, because in the tail everyone is. Nobody is the exception in a crash.

9. Leave that, take this: leave the feeling that the calm will last; take the stop that assumes it will not.
