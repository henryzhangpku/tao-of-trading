# 21. Within the Dim

*Part I: The Way · 《孔德之容》*

## The Verse

孔德之容，惟道是从。道之为物，惟恍惟惚。

惚兮恍兮，其中有象；恍兮惚兮，其中有物。窈兮冥兮，其中有精；其精甚真，其中有信。

自古及今，其名不去，以阅众甫。吾何以知众甫之状哉？以此。

The bearing of great virtue follows the way alone. The way, as a thing, is elusive and indistinct.

Indistinct and elusive, yet within it there is an image. Elusive and indistinct, yet within it there is a thing. Deep and dark, yet within it there is an essence. The essence is very real, and within it there is proof.

From ancient times until now its name has not gone, and by it we see the beginning of all things. How do I know what the beginning of all things is like? By this.

*The Chinese is the received Wang Bi text. The translation is the author's own.*

## The Trader

1. Edge is dim. It does not show in any one trade. A single trade is mostly noise with a little edge stirred in.

2. Within the dim there is an image. Only in the count, over many trades, does the shape come out of the fog.

3. If you can see the edge plainly in one trade, it is not edge. It is a story about one trade.

4. Count every trial. The failed ones belong in the count. A ledger that keeps only the winners is a brochure.

5. Try one idea twenty ways and one of the twenty will look good by luck alone. The ledger knows it was twenty. Your memory will say one.

6. So the bar rises with every try. An idea tried many ways must clear a higher line than an idea tried once.

7. Deep and dark, yet within it there is an essence. You will not see a real edge clearly for a long time. That is not a fault in the edge. It is what edge is like.

8. The essence is very real, and within it there is proof. Proof is two numbers: how many times you looked, and what you found.

9. Its name has not gone. A real edge shows up in the first half of the history and in the second half, separately. A fitted one changes its name every time you cut the data.

10. How do I know? By this. By the count, not by the feeling.
