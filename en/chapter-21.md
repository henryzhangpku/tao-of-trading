# 21. Within the Dim

*Part I: The Way · 《孔德之容》*

## The Verse

孔德之容，惟道是从。道之为物，惟恍惟惚。

惚兮恍兮，其中有象；恍兮惚兮，其中有物。窈兮冥兮，其中有精；其精甚真，其中有信。

自古及今，其名不去，以阅众甫。吾何以知众甫之状哉？以此。

The bearing of great virtue follows the way alone. The way, as a thing, is elusive and indistinct.

Indistinct and elusive, yet within it there is an image. Elusive and indistinct, yet within it there is a thing. Deep and dark, yet within it there is an essence. The essence is very real, and within it there is proof.

From ancient times until now its name has not gone, and by it we see the beginning of all things. How do I know what the beginning of all things is like? By this.

*The Chinese is the received Wang Bi text. The translation is the author's own.*

## What You Know

Do not trade until you are sure. Wait for confirmation: the close above the level, the second candle, the volume coming in, the news that explains it. A trade without conviction is a gamble, and if you are not sure, you stay out. Professionals act when the picture is clear, and they can tell you exactly why the trade has to work. It is the same rule you were given for everything else in life: look before you leap, get the facts first, do not commit until you know. Doubt means wait. Certainty means go.

## The Other Way

1. *The way, as a thing, is elusive and indistinct.* So is every trade worth taking. A clear one is already in the price.

2. *Indistinct and elusive, yet within it there is an image.* Vague is not the same as empty.

3. Certainty arrives late. By the time the picture is clear, the move it describes is half over.

4. Confirmation has a price. Every candle you wait for moves your entry further from the place you would be wrong.

5. *Within it there is a thing.* What is real at the moment of entry is small: a level, a reaction at it, a stop that makes sense.

6. *Deep and dark, yet within it there is an essence.* You can act on the essence without seeing the rest.

7. Size for the dim. A trade you are unsure of is not a trade to skip. It is a trade to take small.

8. *The essence is very real, and within it there is proof.* The proof is not your conviction. It is the price where you would know you were wrong.

9. Demand certainty and you will manufacture it. A trader who must be sure will talk himself into being sure, and then buy at the top of the talking.

10. *How do I know? By this.* Not by being sure. By knowing where it fails.

## The Story

There is a level you have been watching for a week. This morning the price comes down to it. It stops. A little buying shows up, not much. The bars get smaller. Something is happening there, or nothing is. You cannot tell.

So you wait, the way you were taught. You want a close above the morning high. It comes, but on light volume, so you want a second bar. The second bar comes. Now you want to see whether the sector agrees. It does, roughly. By now the stock is two percent off the level, and the picture is clear. You feel it in your chest: this is the trade.

You buy, and because you are sure, you buy more than usual. Your stop goes under the level, which is now a long way below. To keep the risk the same you should have bought less. Certainty does not think like that. Certainty buys more.

The first ordinary pullback takes back half the move. The second takes the rest. You are stopped at the level you had been watching all week, the one where you could have bought with a stop a few cents away, for a loss three times what that trade would have risked. The sure trade was the one that lost.

---

There is another version of the same morning. You wait, you get your confirmation, and by then the stock is so far from the level that buying would be chasing. You do the disciplined thing and stay out. It goes on up all day. You were right about everything and you have nothing.

Both versions start from the same belief: that there is a moment when the dim becomes clear, and that the trade begins there. There is no such moment. There is only a moment when you feel sure, and the feeling comes from the price having already moved.

---

Laozi does not say the way is clear. He says it is *elusive and indistinct*, twice, and then he says that inside it there is something: an image, a thing, an essence, *very real*. He does not wait for the fog to lift before he says it is there.

The other way to sit through that morning starts at the level. The price comes down and stops. You do not know whether it will hold. You know one thing: if it breaks the low of the morning, you are wrong. That is the essence, the small real thing inside the fog. The stop is close because you are early, so a loss would be small. You buy at a size where being wrong is boring.

Many mornings like this, it does not hold, and you lose a little and walk away. On the mornings it does, you are in at the start, and the clarity that arrives an hour later is something you watch from inside the trade instead of something you chase. The confirmation still comes. It just comes to you as information, not as an invitation.

---

Every trader says he wants certainty. What he wants is to be told it is safe. The market does not tell anyone that, at any price. The trader who waits for it gets the worst price and the most confidence, which is the most dangerous pair in trading.

Life works this way too, more than we are told. The job, the move, the decision about a person: by the time you are sure, the moment has usually passed. You can still act in the dim. Act small, know where you would turn back, and let the clarity come later.

## Try This

For one week, before each trade, write down the price where you would know you were wrong, and nothing about why you are sure. Take trades only where that price is close, at a size where being wrong does not matter much. Notice how often you are tempted to wait for one more bar, and watch what changes when you do not.
