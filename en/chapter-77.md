# 77. Bending the Bow

*Part II: The Virtue · 《天之道，其犹张弓与》*

## The Verse

天之道，其犹张弓与？高者抑之，下者举之；有余者损之，不足者补之。

天之道，损有余而补不足。人之道则不然，损不足以奉有余。

孰能有余以奉天下？唯有道者。是以圣人为而不恃，功成而不处，其不欲见贤。

The way of heaven: is it not like stringing a bow? What is high is pressed down, what is low is lifted. What has too much is reduced, what has too little is filled.

The way of heaven takes from what has too much and gives to what has too little. The way of man is not so. It takes from what has too little to serve what has too much.

Who can take what he has too much of and serve the world with it? Only one who has the Way. So the sage acts and does not lean on it. He finishes the work and does not stay in it. He has no wish to show his worth.

*The Chinese is the received Wang Bi text. The translation is the author's own.*

## The Trader

1. Your own results obey the bow. The high stretch is pressed down, the low stretch is lifted. Most of what looks like skill in a hot month and failure in a cold one is the string going back to rest.

2. So the best month of a test is the least trustworthy month in it. Take it out and look at what is left.

3. If nothing is left, there was never a method. There was a month.

4. Do the same with the best single trade, and the best single name. A record that stands only on its tallest tree is one storm from flat ground.

5. The way of man takes from what has too little to serve what has too much. After a hot run, the trader adds size to the thing that just ran, and starves the quiet rules that were doing the ordinary work.

6. Do not raise size because the last stretch was good. Raise it, if ever, because the long record is good and the size was always too small for it.

7. Do not cut a sound rule because the last stretch was cold. Cold stretches are what the string does on the way down.

8. Expect your next month to be closer to your average than your last month was. That is not pessimism. It is the bow.

9. The sage finishes the work and does not stay in it. Close the good month, write it down, and do not move in.

10. He has no wish to show his worth. A trader who needs the best month to be the real one will keep trading until it is proven, and it will not be.
