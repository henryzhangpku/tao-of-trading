# About the Author

**Henry Zhang · 张恒** grew up in China, where he first met the *Tao Te Ching* as a schoolchild, a few lines at a time, in the original. He still reads it that way.

He is a quant engineer. He builds trading systems that run on written rules: rules that are frozen before they are tested, fingerprinted so they cannot be quietly edited, and tested forward on prices nobody has seen, for a season, before anything about them is changed. Every test is counted in a ledger, including the ones that fail. He also runs an AI agent that trades in public on those rules, on one principle that appears all through this book: the model proposes, the rules decide.

That is the reason for this book. Laozi has been read for markets before, most usefully by Mark Spitznagel, who read him in translation for capital over decades. Henry reads him in the original, for the trading day and for rules run by machines, and works at the desk he writes about. He thinks both halves, the old text in its own language and the modern desk, are worth having first-hand in one book, and that he is in a fair position to write it.

He is the author of *The Art of Options War* · 《期权兵法》, Sun Tzu's thirteen chapters for the options trader, of which this book is the companion, and of *The Counted Trade* · 《算而后战》 and *The Options Underwriter* · 《期权承保》. His books are free to read in open editions and on the Henry Zhang Books app. He writes them in English and again, by hand, in Chinese.

None of his books makes a performance claim, and this one is no exception. He will tell you what he did and why, never what it earned.
