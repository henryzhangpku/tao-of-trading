# About the Author

**Henry Zhang · 张恒** grew up in China, where he first met the *Tao Te Ching* as a schoolchild, a few lines at a time, in the original. He still reads it that way.

He is a quant engineer and an active trader. He builds trading systems that run on written rules, and runs an AI agent that trades in public. This book is not about that work. It is about the part of trading that no system removes: the person at the screen, with the same habits of mind everyone is taught, and an old Chinese book that sees each of them the other way round.

Laozi has been read for markets before, most usefully by Mark Spitznagel, who read him in translation for capital over decades. Henry reads him in the original, for the trading day and the trader's mind.

He is the author of *The Art of Options War* · 《期权兵法》, Sun Tzu's thirteen chapters for the options trader, of which this book is the companion, and of *The Counted Trade* · 《算而后战》 and *The Options Underwriter* · 《期权承保》. His books are free to read in open editions and on the Henry Zhang Books app. He writes them in English and again, by hand, in Chinese.

None of his books makes a performance claim, and this one is no exception.
