# 35. Hold the Great Image

*Part I: The Way · 《执大象》*

## The Verse

执大象，天下往。往而不害，安平太。

乐与饵，过客止。道之出口，淡乎其无味，视之不足见，听之不足闻，用之不足既。

Hold the great image, and the world comes to you. It comes and comes to no harm: safety, peace and calm.

Music and good food make the passing traveller stop. But the way, when it leaves the mouth, is bland and has no flavour. Look at it, and there is not enough to see. Listen to it, and there is not enough to hear. Use it, and it cannot be used up.

*The Chinese is the received Wang Bi text. The translation is the author's own.*

## The Trader

1. Good rules are boring to describe. If your best rule makes the table lean forward at dinner, test it again.

2. Music and bait make the traveller stop: the screenshot, the story, the month that changed everything. The way has no flavour.

3. Bland is a feature. A rule with no story attached gives you nothing to fall in love with, and nothing to defend when it is wrong.

4. Look at it, and there is not enough to see: an entry, a size, a stop, an exit. Four lines on a card.

5. Listen to it, and there is not enough to hear. A system doing its job makes very little noise.

6. Use it, and it cannot be used up. The bland rule does not need a new market, a new story or a new season to keep working the way it works.

7. Hold the great image, and the world comes. Hold the plain rule, and the trades arrive on their own schedule, not yours.

8. It comes to no harm. A process that is safe to run every day is worth more than one that is thrilling to run once.

9. If you explain your rule and the table loses interest, you have probably explained it correctly.
