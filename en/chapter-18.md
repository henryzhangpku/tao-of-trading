# 18. When the Great Way Is Lost

*Part I: The Way · 《大道废》*

## The Verse

大道废，有仁义；智慧出，有大伪；六亲不和，有孝慈；国家昏乱，有忠臣。

When the great way is lost, benevolence and righteousness appear. When cleverness comes forth, great falseness appears. When the six relations are out of harmony, filial piety and kindness appear. When the state is in confusion and disorder, loyal ministers appear.

*The Chinese is the received Wang Bi text. The translation is the author's own.*

## The Trader

1. When a system needs many exceptions, the system is broken. The exceptions are not the repair. They are the symptom.

2. A rule that works does not need a speech in its defence. When you hear yourself explaining why this month does not count, the way is already lost.

3. Watch the length of the rule list. Each line was added for a reason, after a loss. A list that only grows is a record of losses, not a rule.

4. When cleverness comes forth, great falseness appears. The clever patch fits the last bad week perfectly. That fit is the falseness.

5. Exceptions breed. "Except before a report" becomes "except before a report, unless the trend is strong," and soon nobody, including you, can say what the rule does.

6. When the family is out of harmony, piety appears. When the trader is out of step with the rule, discipline becomes a topic. Discipline you have to talk about is already failing.

7. Loyal ministers appear in a state in disorder. Heroic trades appear in a system that is not working. A good system has no heroes.

8. Count the exceptions the way you count the trades. When the count keeps rising, do not add another. Retire the rule, write down why, and start again from the plain version.

9. Retiring a rule is not failing. It is the one exception a good system allows.
