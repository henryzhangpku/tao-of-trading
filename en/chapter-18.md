# 18. When the Great Way Is Lost

*Part I: The Way · 《大道废》*

## The Verse

大道废，有仁义；智慧出，有大伪；六亲不和，有孝慈；国家昏乱，有忠臣。

When the great way is lost, benevolence and righteousness appear. When cleverness comes forth, great falseness appears. When the six relations are out of harmony, filial piety and kindness appear. When the state is in confusion and disorder, loyal ministers appear.

*The Chinese is the received Wang Bi text. The translation is the author's own.*

## What You Know

Pressure shows what you are made of. You were taught to admire the comeback: the team down at half-time that wins it in the last minute, the student who pulls the all-nighter and passes, the one who stays calm when everything is on fire. Anyone looks good when it is easy. In trading the real test is the bad morning. You are down hard by eleven, and instead of folding you dig in, fight, and grind your way back to flat or better by the close. That is a professional. Those are the days you remember and tell people about. Any fool can make money on a quiet trending day. The save is what proves you can trade.

## The Other Way

1. *When the great way is lost, benevolence and righteousness appear.* Virtues get names when they go missing. A trader who talks a lot about grit has had to use a lot of it.

2. *When the state is in confusion and disorder, loyal ministers appear.* Heroes appear in disorder. A day that needed a hero was already a bad day before the hero arrived.

3. The save is remembered because it was rare and loud. The day that never needed saving leaves nothing behind to remember, and so it is valued least.

4. To make a save, you had to fall first. The story of the comeback always starts at the low and calls it the beginning.

5. Comebacks are paid for with size and speed. The same habits that rescue one day dig a deeper hole on another, and that day never becomes a story.

6. *When cleverness comes forth, great falseness appears.* Down at eleven, you become very clever: new reasons, new names, trades you would never usually take. The cleverness is the warning.

7. Fighting back treats the morning as an opponent you can beat by four o'clock. The close is not keeping score. A day is not a match you have to win.

8. *When the six relations are out of harmony, filial piety and kindness appear.* When you and your plan have fallen out, you grow loud in your loyalty to it and quiet in following it.

9. The professional does not get back to flat. He keeps the loss the size it already was.

10. A good trading day is one nobody would ever tell a story about. That is the aim.

## The Story

It is Thursday, and by five past ten you are down more than you usually lose in a week. Two trades stopped out in the first twenty minutes, and a third you held too long because you could not stand to take another stop so soon. You feel the shift in your body. You sit forward. Your shoulders come up. A sentence arrives in your head, fully formed: not today.

You know this feeling and you like it. It is the feeling from every comeback story you have ever admired.

---

You double your size on the next trade, because you need to cover more ground now. It works. You take another, a name you do not normally trade, because it is moving and you need movement. That works too. By half past two you are close to flat. At ten to four you close the last position with a small gain for the day. You sit back and breathe out. You feel like a professional. That evening you tell someone about it, and you tell it well: the hole, the fight, the climb out.

The next Thursday the morning goes wrong in the same way, and the same sentence arrives: not today. You double your size. This time it does not work, and the next trade does not either. By the close the hole is three times what it was at ten. That evening you tell nobody.

Over a season, you remember the saves and you do not remember the holes. Your memory keeps the heroes and quietly buries the soldiers. *When the state is in confusion and disorder, loyal ministers appear.* The hero was never proof of a good trader. He was proof of a morning in disorder, and of a trader who answered disorder with more of it.

---

Now the other Thursday. Five past ten, the same three losses, the same weight in your chest.

You sit forward. The sentence arrives: not today. This time you recognise it. It is the loyal minister arriving, and the loyal minister only ever arrives when the state is already in trouble. So you do the dull thing. You write the loss on a pad. Under it you write: no rescue. From now on you trade only your normal setups, at your normal size, or not at all.

The rest of the morning, two things move that you might have chased. You let them go. In the afternoon one of your ordinary setups appears and you take it at normal size. It makes a little back. Then nothing. At four o'clock you are down for the day, by about what you were down at five past ten.

There is no story. You do not tell anyone, because there is nothing to tell. The next morning you start at normal size with a normal head, which is the whole point.

---

*When the state is in confusion and disorder, loyal ministers appear.* We are taught that pressure reveals character and that the comeback is the true test. The old book sees it the other way round. Heroism is a sign of disorder, not a cure for it. Where things are in order, nobody needs saving, and nobody gets the credit.

The best days you will have look as if nothing happened. Nobody will ever ask you about them.

## Try This

For the next month, before each session, write down a number for the day beyond which you are "down". If you reach it, write "no rescue" on a note and put it under the screen: from then on, normal setups at normal size, or nothing. At the end of the month, look at your worst days and see how much of each loss came from the morning and how much from the fight afterwards.
